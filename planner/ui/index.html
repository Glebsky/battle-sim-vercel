Algorithm Settings
General Usage Strategy Maximum (speed): primary goal is to clear the maximum number of camps in a single wave to minimize total waves in the adventure. Solos easy camps and squads hard camps. Minimum (conserve): primary goal is to preserve generals and troops without inflating wave sizes.
Stock Loss Accounting Worst-case (max): deducts maximum damage observed across all simulation runs. Guarantees the army will never run out of troops due to bad RNG. Average (avg): deducts expected statistical average losses.
Sacrificial Armies Cheap: only allows sacrificing cheap units (Recruits, Militia, Swordsmen, Knights). Units marked «no loss» are strictly forbidden.

Any: allows any unprotected units.

Disable: forbids attacks resulting in army wipeouts.
Composition Grid Step (%) Step size when iterating troop ratios in generals' armies. For example, 10% tests compositions in increments of 10%, 20%, 30%...

10% offers the optimal balance of precision and speed.
Screening Simulations Number of battle simulations during candidate screening. Quickly eliminates unviable compositions.
Verification Simulations Number of verification simulations on the final winning army to guarantee 100% win rate and eliminate hidden defeat chances.
Max Generals in Squad Limit the length of a general squad chain per camp. 0 = unlimited (as many as needed to clear the camp).
0 = no limit (all generals)
Beam Search Width Number of best candidate armies retained for the next squad stage. Higher = wider and deeper search.
